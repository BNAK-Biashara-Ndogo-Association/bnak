About BNak / impact

Progress that leaves no one behind.

We organise the everyday economy around dignity, practical knowledge and a healthier Kenya. That means backing the owners already doing the work and making room for the next generation.

A stronger livelihood. A lighter footprint.

Move clean

We promote e-mobility for riders, couriers and local fleets so Kenyan businesses can cut fuel costs and protect the air we share.

Protect what sustains us

From farms to guesthouses, we help MSMEs turn resource care, local sourcing and resilient operations into good business.

Build belonging

A fair economy is designed with young people, women and people with disabilities at the table from the start.

Youth development

Enterprise skills, mentors and first-market opportunities for young Kenyans turning ideas into income.

Women in business

Peer circles and finance-readiness support that recognise women as owners, employers and decision-makers.

Access for all

Accessible learning, inclusive networks and visibility for entrepreneurs with disabilities.
