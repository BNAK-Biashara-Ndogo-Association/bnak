Turnkey Solutions

FIQA Turnkey Solutions provide comprehensive project management and implementation services across a wide range of infrastructure needs, not limited to vertical transportation. Whether it's adding a lift to an existing building, installing escalators or moving walkways in large transit hubs, or enhancing accessibility features across a facility, FIQA takes care of every detail from start to finish. The process includes feasibility studies, design customization, engineering, procurement, installation, and commissioning, ensuring a seamless and efficient experience for clients.

For retrofitting solutions in older buildings, FIQA specializes in designing and integrating systems that comply with modern standards while preserving architectural aesthetics. Beyond elevators and escalators, FIQA also delivers customized solutions for energy efficiency, automated building systems, and user experience enhancements. By addressing challenges such as space constraints, environmental impact, and operational efficiency, FIQA ensures tailored solutions that meet client requirements. These services are ideal for commercial complexes, healthcare facilities, residential buildings, and public infrastructure projects, with a strong focus on delivering quality, reliability, and sustainability.
